Privacy Policy

How Madar Hub collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you contact us, use our space, or manage your membership online.

Last updated: 28 August 2026  ·  Applies to: madarorbit.com and the Madar Hub membership portal

Who we are

Madar Hub is a coworking and business address service operating at KG 42 Street, Kimironko, Gasabo, Kigali, Rwanda. In this policy, “we” and “Madar Hub” mean Madar Hub Ltd, which is responsible for the personal data described here.

For any question about this policy or your personal data, contact us at contact@madarorbit.com or on WhatsApp at 0783 662 543.

What we collect

When you contact us

Our contact form does not send anything to our servers. It opens WhatsApp with a message prepared for you, and you choose whether to send it. If you do, we receive your WhatsApp name, phone number, and whatever you write, and we keep a record of the enquiry so we can answer it and follow up.

When you visit or subscribe

We keep a customer record containing:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your email address, where you give us one
  • Which service or package you are interested in or have bought
  • Visit dates, payment dates, amounts, and payment method
  • Notes our staff make about your enquiry, so you are not asked the same thing twice

When you create a membership account

The membership portal at madarorbit.com/membership stores your email address, your name, your phone number, and a cryptographic hash of your password. We never store your password itself and cannot read it. If you sign in with Google, we also store the account identifier Google gives us so we can recognise you next time.

Automatically, when you browse

Our web servers keep standard access logs containing IP addresses, the pages requested, timestamps, and browser information. These are used for security and to diagnose faults.

What we do not do. We do not use advertising trackers, analytics pixels, or third-party marketing cookies on this website. We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone, and we do not use your information to build advertising profiles.

How we use your information

  • To answer your enquiries and arrange visits
  • To provide the workspace, meeting room, or business address service you have booked
  • To take payment and keep accurate financial records
  • To tell you when a membership payment is due, and to confirm payments received
  • To let you sign in to the membership portal and see your own subscription and payment history
  • To meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations in Rwanda

Payments

Online payments are processed by Pesapal. When you pay online you are taken to Pesapal’s own secure page, and your card number, PIN, or mobile money credentials are entered there, not on our website. We never receive, see, or store those details.

We do receive and store a record of the transaction: the package purchased, the amount, the reference number, the status, and the name, email, and phone number associated with the payment.

Who we share information with

We share personal data only with the service providers we need in order to operate, and only what each one needs:

ProviderPurposeWhat they receive
Pesapal Processing online payments Your name, email, phone, and the amount being paid
Google Sign-in, only if you choose “Continue with Google” Google confirms your email address and name to us
Resend Sending password resets and payment reminders Your email address and the content of that email
Anthropic An AI assistant that helps staff summarise enquiries and draft replies The text of an enquiry conversation, which may include your name and phone number
Cloudflare Website delivery, security, and protection against attacks Your IP address and request details

Some of these providers operate outside Rwanda, which means your data may be processed abroad. We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to establish or defend a legal claim.

If you sign in with Google

Choosing “Continue with Google” is optional; you can always use an email address and password instead. When you use it, Google tells us your email address, your name, and an identifier for your Google account. We use that only to create and sign you into your Madar Hub membership account.

We do not request access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar, files, or any other Google service, and we never post anything on your behalf. You can disconnect Madar Hub at any time from your Google Account’s third-party access settings.

Cookies

We use a single cookie, and only after you sign in to the membership portal. It stores a signed session token so the portal knows you are logged in as you move between pages. It contains no personal information beyond an account reference, and it is deleted when you log out. If you tick “Keep me logged in” it lasts 30 days; otherwise it is removed when you close your browser.

We set no advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies, so this site does not ask you to accept cookies for those purposes.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become customers — kept while there is a realistic prospect of your becoming a member, then deleted on request.
  • Customer and payment records — kept for as long as required by Rwandan tax and accounting law.
  • Membership accounts — kept while your account is open. Ask us to close it and we delete the login, keeping only the financial records we are legally required to hold.
  • Server access logs — kept for a short period for security purposes.
  • Password reset links — expire one hour after being issued.

How we protect it

  • The whole site is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection.
  • Passwords are stored using scrypt, a slow one-way hashing algorithm. They cannot be reversed, and staff cannot read them.
  • Sign-in sessions use signed, HttpOnly cookies that scripts in your browser cannot read.
  • Customer records are behind a staff-only login, separate from member accounts.
  • Card and mobile money credentials never reach our systems at all.

No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but we take these measures seriously and review them as the service changes.

Your rights

Under Rwanda’s Law N° 058/2021 relating to the protection of personal data and privacy, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy
  • Have inaccurate information corrected
  • Ask us to delete your data, where we are not legally required to keep it
  • Object to a particular use of your data, or ask us to restrict it
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where our use relies on your consent
  • Complain to the supervisory authority in Rwanda, the National Cyber Security Authority

Members can view and correct their own name, phone number, and email at any time under Your details in the membership portal. For anything else, write to contact@madarorbit.com and we will respond as quickly as we can.

Children

Our services are intended for adults and for students of secondary school age and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16 without the involvement of a parent or guardian. If you believe we have, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services change. The date at the top always shows when it was last revised, and any significant change will be communicated to members by email or on this page.
