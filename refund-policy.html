Refund Policy

When you can get your money back for a day pass, a room booking, or a membership, and how to ask for it.

Last updated: 29 September 2026  ·  Applies to: payments made on madarorbit.com, in the membership portal, or at the hub

At a glance

ServiceFull refund ifOtherwise
Day passes & rooms You cancel at least 24 hours before your booking No refund, but you can move the booking once, to a date within 30 days
Monthly plans You ask within 48 hours of paying and have not used the service No refund for the month you are in; simply do not renew
6- & 12-month plans You ask within 48 hours of paying and have not used the service We refund what is left after charging the months used at the monthly rate
If we get it wrong, you get everything back. If you are charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or we cannot provide what you paid for, we refund the full amount you were charged, including any online payment fee.

Day passes and room bookings

This covers the Coworking Day Pass, the Student Meeting Room Day Pass, Meeting Room rental (up to 4 or 6 hours), and the Workshops & Training Room.

  • 24 hours’ notice or more — cancel at least 24 hours before your booking starts and we refund you in full.
  • Less than 24 hours, or you do not come — the payment is not refunded, but you can move the booking once, to any available date within 30 days of the original one.
  • Paid, but no date agreed yet — if you paid online and have not yet fixed a date with us, nothing is booked, and you can cancel for a full refund at any time before your visit.
  • Unused hours — rooms are booked as a block, so leaving early or using fewer hours than you booked is not refunded.

Monthly memberships

This covers the Fixed Desk Monthly Subscription, Virtual Address / Business Address Support on the monthly plan, the Fixed Desk + Virtual Address package, and both Private Team Room packages.

You can have a full refund if you ask within 48 hours of paying and have not yet used the service. “Used” means:

  • For a desk or team room, that you or a registered team member have worked from the space.
  • For business address support, that we have given you address authorization, you have used our address on a registration, filing, or official correspondence, or we have received mail on your behalf.

After 48 hours, or once you have used the service, the month you have paid for is not refunded. You keep full access until the end of it. The same rule applies to each renewal payment.

6- and 12-month Virtual Address plans

The same 48-hour rule applies: ask within 48 hours of paying, before you have used the service, and we refund you in full.

After that you can still cancel at any time. The lower monthly price on these plans is given in return for committing to the whole term, so if you leave early we charge the months you have had at the standard monthly rate of 118,000 RWF including VAT, count the month you are in as used, and refund the rest.

For example, if you paid 1,020,000 RWF for a 12-month plan and cancel during your fourth month, four months at 118,000 RWF comes to 472,000 RWF, so we refund 548,000 RWF.

If the months you have used come to as much as you paid, or more, there is nothing left to refund, and you never owe us anything extra. In practice that point is reached in the sixth month of a 6-month plan and the ninth month of a 12-month plan.

Ending a membership

We never charge you automatically. Every payment is one you make yourself, by MoMo, card, or at the hub, so to end a membership you only need to stop renewing. We send a reminder when a payment is due, and a membership that is still unpaid 7 days after its due date is suspended.

It still helps to tell us you are leaving, so we can free up your desk or room. If your plan included business address support, our address and mail handling stop when it ends, and you are responsible for updating your business address with RDB, RRA, your bank, and anyone else who holds it.

Online payment fees

MTN MoMo adds no fee, so a MoMo refund is the full amount you paid.

Paying by card, bank, or other mobile money through Pesapal adds a 3% online payment fee at checkout. That fee is charged by Pesapal and is not refundable: we refund the price of the service including VAT. For example, if you paid 121,540 RWF for a 118,000 RWF membership, the refund is 118,000 RWF.

The only exception is a mistake on our side, described next, where we refund the fee as well.

When we refund in full

Whatever the timing, you get back everything you were charged, including any fee, if:

  • You were charged twice for the same thing, or charged more than the listed price
  • We cancel your booking, or cannot provide the space or service you paid for. You can choose a refund or a new date.
  • Money left your account but the payment shows as failed or never reached us. We check with Pesapal or MTN and either confirm your booking or refund you in full.

What we cannot refund

  • Days, hours, or months already used, except as set out above for 6- and 12-month plans.
  • The outcome of a government or institutional process. Madar Hub provides address authorization, mail handling, and office support, but decisions on RDB or RRA registration, tax, bank, and legal matters are made by those institutions. A refusal is not grounds for refunding a service we have already provided.

How to ask for a refund

Message us on WhatsApp at 0783 662 543 or email contact@madarorbit.com with:

  • Your name, and the phone number or email address you paid with
  • Your payment reference, shown on the payment confirmation page and in your Pesapal receipt, or the transaction ID in your MoMo SMS. Members can also see their payments in the membership portal.
  • What you would like to cancel

We will tell you whether a refund is due, and how much, normally within two working days.

How refunds are paid

  • MTN MoMo — back to the MoMo number you paid from.
  • Card, bank, or other mobile money via Pesapal — back through Pesapal to the card or account you paid with.
  • Cash or bank transfer at the hub — by MoMo or bank transfer to the person who paid.

We do not send refunds to a different person or account, which protects you if someone else tries to claim your payment. Approved refunds are sent within 7 working days. Card refunds can then take a further 5 to 10 working days to appear, depending on your bank.

Your legal rights

This policy does not affect any rights you have under Rwandan consumer protection law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services change. The date at the top always shows when it was last revised, and a payment is always handled under the policy that was in place when you made it.
