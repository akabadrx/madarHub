Refund & Cancellation Policy

When you can cancel a booking, membership, address plan, or course, what you get back, and how to ask for it.

Last updated: 15 October 2026  ·  Applies to: every Madar Hub and Badr Academy service, paid online, by Mobile Money, or at the hub

1. Scope

This policy sets out when and how clients of MADAR HUB LTD can cancel a booking, subscription, or enrolment, and what is refunded. It applies to every service sold under the Madar Hub and Badr Academy brands, whether paid online, by Mobile Money, or in person.

2. General principles

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 14 business days of approval. The rules below apply to every service unless a later section or a signed contract says otherwise.

  1. Confirm before you pay. Clients must confirm availability with Madar Hub before paying. If we cannot provide a service paid for without confirmation, the client receives a full refund.
  2. Signed contracts prevail. Where a client has a signed contract (private office, address plan, corporate booking), the contract terms override this policy.
  3. Refund method. Refunds are paid to the original method only: Mobile Money to the paying number, card payments back to the card through our payment provider. We do not refund to a third party’s account.
  4. Processing time. We acknowledge a request within 2 business days and decide within 5 business days. Approved refunds are sent within 14 business days. Card refunds may take longer to appear, depending on the client’s bank.
  5. Transaction fees. Payment-processor and Mobile Money charges are deducted from refunds where the cancellation is the client’s choice. They are not deducted when Madar Hub cancels. Pesapal’s 3% online payment fee is explained below.
  6. VAT and invoices. Prices for taxable services include or add 18% VAT as shown on the invoice. Refunds are paid in full, VAT included: the client gets back everything paid for the refunded part of the service. Every refund is issued with a matching EBM refund receipt (credit note).
  7. Credit instead of cash. Where this policy offers credit, the client may use it for any Madar Hub service within 3 months. Credit has no cash value and cannot be transferred.
  8. Written requests only. Cancellations count from the time a written request reaches contact@madarorbit.com or our WhatsApp line, +250 783 662 543. Verbal notice is not enough.

Pesapal’s 3% online payment fee

MTN MoMo payments carry no checkout fee. Paying by card, bank, or other mobile money through Pesapal adds a 3% online payment fee at checkout, which is charged by Pesapal, not by Madar Hub.

Pesapal keeps this fee when a payment is refunded, so it is not refundable. When you cancel, we refund the price of the service including VAT, without the 3% fee. For example, if you paid 121,540 RWF for a 118,000 RWF membership, the refund is 118,000 RWF.

If the refund is because of a mistake on our side, or because Madar Hub cancelled, we pay the 3% fee back ourselves, so you get back everything you were charged.

4. Business Address plans (registered business address)

This covers Virtual Address / Business Address Support on the monthly, 6-month, and 12-month plans. Monthly plans cannot be paused or refunded for the current month; 6-month and 12-month plans are refunded for unused months, with used months charged at the monthly rate.

Monthly plan (100,000 RWF + 18% VAT = 118,000 RWF)

  • There is no automatic renewal. Each month is paid separately and must be paid within 7 days of its due date (see section 8).
  • To stop, tell us in writing before the next due date and do not renew.
  • The current month is not refunded once the address has been issued.
  • The plan cannot be paused. A client who stops must change its registered address on the RDB portal and send us the amendment within 14 days.

6-month plan (540,000 RWF + 18% VAT = 637,200 RWF paid upfront, or 106,200 RWF/month VAT inclusive)

  • Cancellation before the address is issued: full refund, VAT included, less transaction fees.
  • Early cancellation after issue: each month used, including the month of cancellation, is charged at the monthly rate of 118,000 RWF. The balance is refunded in full, VAT included.
  • If the used months at the monthly rate reach or exceed 637,200 RWF (month 6), no refund is due and nothing more is owed.

  • Kids programs: a session missed because of a child’s illness, with notice before the session starts, can be made up in the next intake.

Online memory courses (kids, juniors and adults)

  • Full refund within 7 days of purchase if no more than 20% of the course content has been accessed.
  • No refund after 7 days or once more than 20% has been accessed.

Workshops and events

  • Paid tickets: full refund up to 48 hours before the event; after that, no refund, but the ticket can be transferred to another person.
  • Free events: no refund applies; we ask attendees to cancel so others can take the seat.

If Madar Hub cancels or reschedules a course, session, or event, the client chooses between a full refund (with no fees deducted) or a place on the new date.

7. Food and beverage

Cafe items are not refunded once served. If an item is wrong or not up to standard, tell staff straight away and we will replace it or refund it on the spot.

8. Cancellation or suspension by Madar Hub

If Madar Hub cannot deliver a paid service, the client gets a full refund or credit of their choice; if the client breaks the rules or does not pay, the membership may be cancelled without refund.

Service we cannot deliver. If we close, cancel, or cannot provide a service for reasons within our control, the unused portion is refunded in full with no fees deducted.

Events beyond our control. For extended power, water, or internet outages, government orders, or similar events, we offer credit or a rescheduled date first. If the service cannot be delivered within 30 days, the client may request a refund of the unused portion.

Missed payments (monthly members and address plans). Automatic renewal is not supported through Pesapal at the moment, so each payment must be made by the client.

  1. Madar Hub sends a reminder when the payment is due.
  2. The client has a maximum of 7 days after the due date to pay.
  3. If the payment is still not made after those 7 days, the membership is cancelled and access ends.
  4. Address-plan clients must then amend their registered address on the RDB portal to a new address and send us confirmation of the amendment within a further 7 days.
  5. If we do not receive that confirmation within those 7 days, Madar Hub will contact RDB to inform them that the business is using our address without authorisation.

Breach of house rules. Access may be ended immediately for serious misconduct, damage, illegal activity, or misuse of the Madar Hub address. In that case no refund is due for the current billing cycle, and any outstanding amounts remain payable.

9. How to request a refund

Send a written request to contact@madarorbit.com or WhatsApp +250 783 662 543 with the details below; the request is dated from when we receive it.

  • Full name or business name on the invoice
  • Invoice or EBM receipt number, or your payment reference (shown on the payment confirmation page, in your Pesapal receipt, or as the transaction ID in your MoMo SMS)
  • Service and booking date
  • Reason for cancellation
  • Refund destination (must match the original payment method)
  • Address-plan clients only: proof of the RDB address change

Disputes. If a client disagrees with a decision, they may reply within 14 days and the request will be reviewed by the Managing Director. This policy does not limit any rights the client has under Rwandan consumer protection law. It is governed by the laws of the Republic of Rwanda.

Changes to this policy. Madar Hub may update this policy. Changes apply to purchases made after the update date; existing bookings and subscriptions keep the terms in force when they were paid.
